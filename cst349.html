CST 349 - Computer Science Proseminar

Course Description: The Proseminar course helps students prepare for ‘life after college’ by connecting their major to their broader personal and professional goals. In this course, students will critically analyze information from diverse sources, including academic and business literature and conversations with industry mentors. They will also develop crucial problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills throughout the course. This analysis and skill development will guide them in crafting their personal value proposition and a plan for developing a professional portfolio.
